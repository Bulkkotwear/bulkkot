불꽃

LESS NOISE. MORE PRESENCE.

BULKKOT is a Korean-inspired premium everyday wear label built around proportion, weight and restraint.

We believe clothing does not need excessive graphics or unnecessary noise to create an impression. The silhouette does the talking.

Every collection is developed around pieces that can live beyond a single trend — heavyweight essentials, relaxed proportions and a visual language that stays intentional.
