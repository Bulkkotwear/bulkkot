Our Story

ABOUT
BULKKOT

BULKKOT is a Korean-inspired premium everyday wear brand built for a new generation in India.

Inspired by the clean aesthetics of Korean fashion and international streetwear, BULKKOT focuses on oversized silhouettes, thoughtful design and everyday comfort.

Our goal is simple: to make premium-looking, internationally inspired clothing feel more accessible without compromising the way it looks, feels or is presented.

BULKKOT / OUR STORY

ABOUT BULKKOT 불꽃에 대하여

BULKKOT is a Korean-inspired premium everyday wear brand built for a new generation in India.

Inspired by the clean aesthetics of Korean fashion and international streetwear, BULKKOT focuses on oversized silhouettes, thoughtful design and everyday comfort.

We believe premium-looking clothing should not feel unreachable. Our goal is to make internationally inspired everyday wear more accessible without compromising the way it looks, feels or is presented.

From the way a piece fits to the way it is photographed, packaged and presented, every part of BULKKOT is built around the same idea:

Less noise. More presence.
