OUR STORY

BULKKOT (불꽃) is a Korean-inspired premium everyday wear brand built for the new generation in India.

We believe premium clothing should not feel unreachable or overpriced.

Our approach is simple: clean design, comfortable fits, quality materials and timeless everyday essentials.

Inspired by Korean aesthetics and modern streetwear, BULKKOT is built around pieces that can live beyond a single season or trend.

Less noise. More presence.
